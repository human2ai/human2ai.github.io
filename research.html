01 / CAUSAL REASONING
ACCEPTED · 5 WORKSHOPSORAL + SPOTLIGHT

My Graph Doesn’t Help! Analyzing Causal Graph Construction, Representation, and Utility in LLMs

Aman Syed · Benjamin Li

A model can reconstruct a causal graph faithfully and still reason worse with it. We ask what makes a representation useful—not merely correct.

KEY FINDING

On a 3,461-question evaluation across five models, automatically constructed target-centered causal structure reduced accuracy. Better graph reconstruction did not consistently translate into better reasoning.

3,461evaluation questions
05models analyzed
160controlled benchmark questions

NeurIPS 2026 Workshops: LIGHT (Oral, Paris); LP4FM (Spotlight, Sydney); NewInML (Poster, Paris); PTA and TAE (Posters, Sydney).

Read study summary +

Causal reasoning from narrative text requires large language models (LLMs) to recover causal structure and use it effectively. Explicit causal directed acyclic graphs (DAGs) provide a natural representation of such structure, yet a faithful graph may not yield better downstream reasoning. On the hard section of CausalProbe-2024, augmenting 3,461 questions with automatically constructed target-centered causal structure reduces accuracy across all five models. We also evaluate eight four-variable DAG topologies across 32 contexts and 160 questions, comparing direct reasoning, ground-truth graphs, edge-wise construction, and holistic generation. Our results separate graph reconstruction fidelity from downstream utility: higher reconstruction fidelity is not consistently accompanied by better reasoning, and causal-role annotations can change graph usefulness even when the underlying structure stays the same.

02 / AFFECTIVE DYNAMICS
SUBMITTED · UNDER REVIEWICLR 2027 MAIN CONFERENCE

A Functional Framework for Modeling and Predicting Affective Dynamics in LLMs

Aman Syed · Maheen Rassell · Benjamin Li

Rather than treating emotional tone as a property of one response, we model how it changes across a conversation.

RESEARCH DIRECTION

We model valence, arousal, and dominance as a transition process. Current user affect is the strongest predictor in our experiments, while a model's recent responses add predictive value.

24five-turn scenarios
03LLMs from different developers
VADthree affect dimensions

Status: ICLR 2027 main-conference submission; under review. Not an acceptance.

Read study summary +

Affect in conversation changes over time, yet limited work has disentangled the influence of current user affect from a model’s own recent history. We frame this as a system identification problem over Valence–Arousal–Dominance (VAD) states, learning a transition function for each LLM. Across 24 five-turn scenarios and 18 trajectory families per VAD dimension, we evaluate three LLMs from different developers and replicate within three GPT-5.6 capability tiers. Current user affect is more predictive than previous model-response affect alone, but recent response history still provides held-out predictive value. Nonlinear models improve only modestly over a linear formulation, with a second conversational lag providing a smaller residual signal.

03 / AGENTIC SYSTEMS
WORKSHOP SUBMISSIONGENERALIZED EXTENSION IN PROGRESS

No, I’m Sticking to My Reliable Evidence: An Analysis of Premature Closure in Agentic Systems for Hypothesis Formation

Aman Syed · Benjamin Li · Maheen Rassell · Raghav Balamurugan

When several AI agents evaluate evidence, what makes one agent stop revising its hypothesis too soon?

RESEARCH QUESTION

We investigate how evidence reliability and agent design influence willingness to revise hypotheses during multi-agent scientific reasoning.

Status: Earlier biological-hypothesis study submitted to the NeurIPS 2026 AgenticLS workshop. A broader, non-biological extension is in development. No workshop acceptance is claimed.

About this line of work +

This research asks whether high-confidence evidence can cause AI agents to stick too strongly to their initial hypotheses rather than integrating complementary information from collaborators. In the workshop version, agents exchange structured criticism and revise their hypotheses in a controlled biological hypothesis task. The team is extending this question beyond biology; that extension is not yet presented as an accepted or published paper.
