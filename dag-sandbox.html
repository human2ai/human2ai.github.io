HOW TO INTERPRET

Common causal structures

Fork: has two or more outgoing arrows.

Collider: has two or more incoming arrows.

Intermediate: has at least one incoming and one outgoing arrow.

Mediator (X→Y): lies on a directed path from selected X to Y.

Confounder (X–Y): has paths to X and Y, with a path to Y that does not run through X.

Reachability: counts direct and multi-step directed paths between ordered pairs.

This educational tool identifies structural patterns; causal validity, confounding adjustment, and statistical effects depend on additional assumptions.
