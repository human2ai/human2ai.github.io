HOW TO INTERPRET

Common causal structures

Confounder: a common ancestor of the chosen exposure and outcome.

Mediator: lies along a directed path from exposure to outcome.

Collider: directly receives arrows from at least two other variables. Conditioning on one can introduce bias.

Reachability: counts ordered pairs connected by at least one directed path, even when no direct edge exists.

This educational tool identifies structural patterns; causal validity, confounding adjustment, and statistical effects depend on additional assumptions.
